Welcome to our Church

Welcome to Hartland Baptist Church! We’re delighted to have you here. At HBC, our mission is rooted in the greatest commandments: to love God with all our heart, soul, mind, and strength, and to love our neighbors as ourselves. We are a community of believers committed to living out this mission in everything we do.

Our passion is to go and make disciples, sharing the transformative love of Jesus Christ with all whom we encounter. As we gather together in worship, fellowship, and service, we seek to grow deeper in our faith and reach out to those in need, both locally and globally.

Join us as journey together in faith, hope, and love, seeking to glorify God and make a difference in the world.
