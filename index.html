Hartland Christian School

our purpose

At Hartland Christian School, our purpose is clear: to provide a nurturing environment where students can grow academically, spiritually, and socially. Grounded in the belief that ALL truth comes from God, we are committed to integrating faith into every aspect of education.

Our goal is to prepare students for a life centered around Christ, empowering them to reach their full potential in college, careers, and beyond. Through our curriculum, which utilizes the Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) pace system, students receive a comprehensive education from grades 1 through 12. Additionally, we offer extracurricular activities such as sports, art, music and more.

At Hartland Christian School, we are dedicated to guiding and equipping students for success in all areas of life.
